M7 STUDIO EDITOR - QUICK START
==============================
EXPERIMENTAL BUILD: not yet verified on a physical Windows PC. Please follow
TESTING-CHECKLIST.txt and report what works. Your original photos are never changed.

1. Open "M7 Studio Editor" from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.
   A small black window opens. Leave it open while you edit; closing it quits M7.
   Your web browser opens the editor automatically (address: http://127.0.0.1:7777).
   If the browser does not open, type that address into it yourself.

2. Click "Upload photo" (or drag a photo onto the page).
   Supported: Sony ARW (and other RAW files), JPEG, PNG, TIFF.
   Big RAW files take a little while to open the first time.

3. Type what you want in the box at the bottom left, for example:
     cinematic sports at 80%
     lift the shadows a bit and make it warmer
     crop 4:5
     high contrast black and white
     undo
   Or click one of the ten M7 preset buttons.

4. Drag the yellow line over the photo to compare BEFORE and AFTER.
   Click any line in the History list to go back to that version.

5. Click "Export JPEG" or "Export 16-bit TIFF" to save the full-resolution result.

WHERE ARE MY FILES?
  Pictures\M7 Studio\exports   <- your finished photos
  Pictures\M7 Studio\uploads   <- read-only copies of what you uploaded
  Your original photos are never changed.

CHECK THAT YOUR PC WORKS WITH M7
  Drag a photo onto "Test my photos (self-test).bat". It renders the photo with your
  graphics card and prints PASS/FAIL, the GPU used and the time taken.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  The error message in the chat says what failed. Full details are saved in
  Pictures\M7 Studio\logs  (m7.log and engine-failure-*.txt). Updating the
  graphics driver fixes most render errors.

PRIVACY
  Everything runs on this computer. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  (Optional AI masks and AI noise reduction download free models the first time
  they are used. Those features have not been tested yet.)

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because this app is not code-signed.
Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
